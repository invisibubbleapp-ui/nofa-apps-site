Yes. Invisibubble runs as an overlay on top of other apps, so the bubble and the timer stay on screen while you use apps like YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels. It does not connect to those apps or change them, and it is not affiliated with YouTube, Instagram or their owners.

## How the overlay works

Android lets an app draw a small window on top of other apps once you allow "Display over other apps". Invisibubble uses this to keep its bubble at the edge of the screen. When your time is up, the full-screen takeover also appears on top of the app you are using.

## What "works with" means here

- **The timer counts the minutes you set.** You set it before you start scrolling. It is the same timer whichever app you open.
- **No sign-in to other apps.** Invisibubble does not need your YouTube or Instagram account.
- **No changes to other apps.** The bubble sits on top. The app underneath works as normal.

## Limits to know about

- **Android only.** There is no iPhone version.
- **The overlay permission must stay on.** If you turn off "Display over other apps", the bubble cannot show.
- **Some screens hide overlays.** Android may hide apps that draw on top during some system screens, such as certain permission and settings screens. This is normal Android behaviour.
- **Battery settings on some phones.** Some phone makers limit apps in the background to save battery. Invisibubble can ask you to let it run without battery restrictions. You choose, and you can say no. If the bubble disappears, check the battery settings for Invisibubble on your phone.

## FAQ

### Does Invisibubble block YouTube or Instagram?

No. Invisibubble does not block or change other apps. When your time is up, it shows a full-screen takeover on top, and you choose "I'm done" or add extra time.

### Do I need to connect my YouTube or Instagram account?

No. Invisibubble does not need your YouTube or Instagram account. It is a timer that shows on top of other apps.

### Does it work with other scrolling apps?

Invisibubble shows on top of other apps in the same way, so while the bubble is on, the timer keeps running whichever app you use. Some Android system screens may hide overlays.

{{app.name}} is an Android app that keeps a small floating glass bubble at the edge of your screen, over other apps. You set a scroll timer before you start scrolling, and when time is up a full-screen takeover asks if you are done or want extra time.

{{> playButton}}

## How it works in 3 steps

:::steps
1. **The bubble sits at the edge.** A small glass bubble stays at the edge of your screen, on top of whatever app you are using.
2. **You set the minutes.** Before you start scrolling, tap the bubble and choose "Scroll timer". Pick 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or set a custom time.
3. **The time's up screen.** When the time ends, a full-screen takeover appears. Tap "I'm done", or add extra time.
:::

## More features

- **Extra time with a pause to think.** You can add extra time when the timer ends. The second extension asks you to type a reason first.
- **Reminders on the bubble.** {{app.reminders}}
- **Hide bubble.** The "Hide bubble" option hides the bubble for a time you choose.

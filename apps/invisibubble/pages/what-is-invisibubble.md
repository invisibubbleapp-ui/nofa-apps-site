{{app.name}} is an Android app that keeps a small floating glass bubble at the edge of your screen while you use other apps. You set a scroll timer before you start scrolling, and when the time is up a full-screen takeover asks if you are done or want extra time.

It is made by {{app.developer}} and is available on [Google Play]({{app.playUrl}}). It is a timer you set yourself. It is not an app blocker and it is not a health product.

## The bubble

The bubble is small and made to look like glass. It stays at the edge of the screen, on top of other apps. Android calls this kind of window an overlay. To show it, the app needs the "Display over other apps" permission. The [permissions page]({{url:is-it-safe-and-what-permissions}}) explains this.

## How a scroll session works

1. Before you start scrolling, you set a timer. {{app.openTimer}} You can choose 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or a custom time.
2. You open the app you want to use and scroll as normal. The bubble stays at the edge.
3. When the time is up, a full-screen takeover appears on top of the app.

{{app.timerRuns}}

## What happens when time is up

The full-screen takeover gives you two choices:

- **"I'm done".** Choose this when you are finished.
- **Add extra time.** Choose this to keep going for longer.

The second time you add extra time, the app asks you to type a reason first. This gives you a short pause to decide if you really want to keep going.

## Hiding the bubble

The "Hide bubble" option hides the bubble for a time you choose. This is useful when you need the whole screen clear.

## FAQ

### Is Invisibubble an app blocker?

No. Invisibubble is a timer you set yourself before you scroll. When the time is up, a full-screen takeover lets you choose "I'm done" or add extra time.

### Can I set my own timer length?

Yes. You can choose 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or set a custom time.

### What does the colour ring on the bubble mean?

A coloured ring on the bubble shows that you have a reminder for the same day. There is no reminder notification.

### Can I hide the bubble?

Yes. The "Hide bubble" option hides the bubble for a time you choose.

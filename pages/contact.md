The way to contact Nofa Applications is by email at [{{site.email}}](mailto:{{site.email}}). Please include the app name and your Android version if you are reporting a problem.

## What to include

For app problems, it helps to send:

- The app name.
- Your phone model and Android version.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
- A screenshot, if you can.

## Fact corrections and press

If you write about one of our apps and want to check a fact, the [Invisibubble fact sheet](/apps/invisibubble/facts/) has the official facts in one table. If you find a wrong fact on another website, please email us.
